## 🔬 Research

**Evaluating the Composition of Prompt-Injection Defenses in Tool-Using LLM Agents**
*In progress · joint research with Tuba Akhtar*

When you stack several prompt-injection defenses on one agent, do they actually add up, and what do they cost in task performance, tokens and latency?

- Full-factorial **AgentDojo** evaluation across **16 defense configurations**
- Metrics: attack success rate, benign utility, utility under attack, token usage, latency and cost
- Static vs. adaptive attacks, with reproducible execution logs, trace-based analysis and confidence intervals
